Unit 1: Practice Quiz

Information

Ungraded self-check — use this to prepare for Quiz 1. Answer key is at the bottom; don't peek until you've tried every question. Covers the same skills as the real quiz: ASCII, binary conversion, binary addition, binary multiplication, and Scratch block tracing.

  1. Using the ASCII chart, what is the decimal representation of "cat"?
  2. [ ] A. cat
  3. [ ] B. 99 97 116
  4. [ ] C. 97 99 116

  5. [ ] D. (it can't be represented as decimals)

  6. Convert binary 11010 to decimal: _

  7. Convert decimal 19 to binary: _

  8. Convert binary 01100 to decimal: _

  9. Add the following binary numbers, showing your work: 

      1001
+ 0111

  10. Both numbers below are in binary. Select the decimal representation of the answer. 110 × 101

  11. [ ] A. 30
  12. [ ] B. 11110
  13. [ ] C. 15

  14. [ ] D. 25

  15. A Scratch program contains the following blocks: 

    when [green flag] clicked
repeat (5)
  move (50) steps
  turn (72) degrees
    What path does the sprite trace?

  16. [ ] A. A straight line
  17. [ ] B. A triangle
  18. [ ] C. A pentagon
  19. [ ] D. A circle

Answer Key

  1. B. 99 97 116 — ASCII: c=99, a=97, t=116

  2. 26 — 11010 = 16+8+0+2+0

  3. 10011 — 19 in binary

  4. 12 — 01100 = 0+8+4+0+0

  5. 10000 — 1001 (9) + 0111 (7) = 16

  6. A. 30 — 110 (6) × 101 (5) = 30 decimal (B is the binary form, not decimal)

  7. C. A pentagon — repeat(5): move + turn 72° five times traces a 5-sided shape (5 × 72 = 360°)

Study tip: if you missed any binary conversion or arithmetic question, go back to Unit 1 Chapter 2 and 3 and redo the division/place-value steps by hand — don't just memorize this quiz's specific numbers.
